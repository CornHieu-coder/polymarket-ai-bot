# Current Research Hypotheses

The research programme is deliberately divided into three hypotheses. H1, H2, and H3 are separate experiments and must not be collapsed into one opaque "AI trading bot" test. Later hypotheses depend on the earlier evidence described below.

## H1 — Forecasting

Determine whether an AI forecasting system can produce probabilities that outperform contemporaneous prediction-market probabilities on suitable markets.

## H2 — Edge monetisation

If forecasting edge exists, determine whether a proper mathematical betting strategy can convert that predictive advantage into positive returns after realistic execution effects.

## H3 — Improved sizing

Only after H1 and H2 are supported, investigate whether a learned or AI-assisted position-sizing and risk policy improves risk-adjusted performance relative to the proper-betting baseline.
